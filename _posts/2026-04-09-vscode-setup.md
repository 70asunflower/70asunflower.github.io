## Productivity Tips

1. **Learn keyboard shortcuts** — `Ctrl+P` for quick file open
2. **Use multi-cursor** — `Alt+Click` or `Ctrl+Alt+↑/↓`
3. **Integrated terminal** — `` Ctrl+` `` to toggle
4. **Snippet shortcuts** — Create custom snippets for repeated patterns

> A well-configured editor saves hours every week.
