## Core Idea

> You do not rise to the level of your goals. You fall to the level of your systems.
> — James Clear

The book argues that **systems > goals**. Instead of setting ambitious goals, build small, sustainable habits.

## The Four Laws of Behavior Change

| Law | Make it... | Example |
|-----|-----------|---------|
| 1st | Obvious | Put your gym clothes by the bed |
| 2nd | Attractive | Pair with something you enjoy |
| 3rd | Easy | Start with 2 minutes of exercise |
| 4th | Satisfying | Track your streak |

## My Takeaways

- **1% better every day** compounds to 37x in a year
- **Habit stacking**: "After I [current habit], I will [new habit]"
- **Environment design** beats willpower every time
- Focus on **identity**, not outcomes: "I am a runner" vs "I want to run a marathon"

## Applying This to Coding

1. **Obvious** — Open your project editor first thing in the morning
2. **Attractive** — Work on the fun part first
3. **Easy** — Commit to just 10 minutes
4. **Satisfying** — Use a commit streak tracker
